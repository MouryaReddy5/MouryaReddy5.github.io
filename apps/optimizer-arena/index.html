About Muon: Muon keeps a Nesterov momentum buffer and replaces it with its nearest orthogonal matrix via five Newton–Schulz iterations before stepping. Here the parameters are a 1×2 matrix, so orthogonalizing it amounts to normalizing the step to roughly unit length: Muon moves at a near-constant speed in the momentum direction, independent of the gradient's scale. On real weight matrices it does the same for every singular direction at once.
