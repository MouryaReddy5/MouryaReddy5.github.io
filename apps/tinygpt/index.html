TinyGPT Writer

A real decoder-only transformer (token + position embeddings, causal multi-head self-attention, MLP, LayerNorm, weight-tied output) trained from scratch in this tab with Adam on your GPU via WebGL. Give it text, press train, and ask it to write.

3 · Write

Train for a few hundred steps, then generate. Untrained output is random characters.

Loss is cross-entropy in nats per character. A uniform guess scores ln(vocab); below about 1.5 the text starts to look like words.
